About Bhuda Kedar

Bhuda Kedar is a beautiful spiritual destination located in Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand. Famous for the ancient Budha Kedar Temple, scenic mountains, rich culture and warm hospitality, it offers travelers a peaceful escape from busy city life.
